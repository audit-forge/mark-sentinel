## 1. Scope
This retention policy covers AI interaction data, AI conversation history, and AI
inference logs generated by M. F. Dynamics AI systems (primarily HASH on [THIS DEVICE]).

## 2. Retention period and schedule
- **AI interaction / conversation data:** retained for a maximum of **90 days**, then purged.
- **AI inference activity logs** (`.activity.db`, `usage-tracker.json`): retained **90 days** for audit, then rolled off.
- **Daily activity log files:** retained **30 days**.
- Compliance and audit records: retained **7 years** as required for regulatory evidence.

## 3. Data lifecycle and expiration
AI interaction data follows a defined lifecycle: created → active use → archived →
expired → deleted. Data reaching its retention limit expires and is removed on a
scheduled basis.

## 4. Deletion / erasure / purge process
- Automated purge runs on the retention schedule above; records older than the
  retention period are deleted (erasure) from the local stores.
- Manual purge can be triggered by the responsible party at any time.

## 5. Right to erasure
Individuals may request **erasure** ("right to be forgotten") of their personal
data held in AI interaction records. Such requests are honoured within 30 days by
the responsible party, who deletes the matching records and confirms completion.
