## 1. Purpose
This AI usage policy defines how artificial-intelligence tools and services may be
used within [YOUR ORGANISATION], so that AI use is safe, compliant, and auditable.

## 2. Approved AI tools and services
The following AI tools are approved for business use:
- **HASH** — local AI agent (Ollama / qwen3, with escalation to Anthropic Claude for hard tasks).
- **Anthropic Claude** (Opus / Sonnet) — via API, for escalated reasoning and coding.
- **Arckon (RiskRaven Arckon)** — AI security & compliance auditing.

Any AI tool not on this approved list must be reviewed and authorised by the
responsible party before use.

## 3. Prohibited uses and prohibited data
Prohibited AI uses include:
- Submitting client personally identifiable information (PII), credentials, secrets,
  or regulated data to any third-party AI service.
- Using AI output as the sole basis for a legal, financial, or compliance decision
  without human review.
- Circumventing security controls, rate limits, or logging.

## 4. Data classification
All data handled by AI systems is classified as **Public**, **Internal**,
**Confidential**, or **Restricted**. Confidential and Restricted data must never
be sent to a third-party AI provider; it may only be processed by the local
HASH model on [THIS DEVICE].

## 5. Responsibilities
The responsible AI owner (above) maintains this policy, the AI asset inventory,
the data-retention policy, and the incident-response plan, and reviews them at
least every 12 months.
